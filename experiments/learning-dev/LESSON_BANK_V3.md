## Fixed decision rule

The primary score is model calls needed for a verified repair. A task that fails within its allowance receives a **36-call penalty**, even if it stopped sooner. This is a repair-effort utility score, **not an actual request count or dollar charge**. A successful task requires Hive's SATISFIED decision plus the immutable final tests passing. API/transport or workspace-integrity failures invalidate confirmation and are not silently discarded.

Confirmation requires every one of the 24 lesson-arm transfer tasks and both lesson-arm retention tasks to pass. Mean penalized effort must fall by at least 15% against **each** control. The exact one-sided paired sign-flip test of the mean difference must have p ≤ .0125 against **each** control. Integer dynamic programming enumerates the full sign distribution without Monte Carlo error. Report all per-task data, control success counts, raw calls, input/output tokens, actual measured-usage charge bounds, development overhead, and failed or interrupted episodes separately.

Using the mean, rather than V2's sign test of smaller call counts, reflects the magnitude of expensive repair detours across heterogeneous tasks. Counting unsuccessful repairs at their full allowance prevents a quick wrong answer from appearing efficient. Both choices are frozen prospectively here; they do not revise the V2 verdict.
