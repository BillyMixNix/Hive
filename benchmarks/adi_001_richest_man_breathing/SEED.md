# I Became the World’s Richest Man by Breathing

## Genre

Modern Wuxia / Urban Cultivation / Progression Fantasy / Economic Power Fantasy / Comedy that gradually becomes frighteningly serious

## Core Premise

Twenty-six-year-old Ren Fujitsu is broke.

Not temporarily embarrassed millionaire broke.

His checking account has $43.17 in it, his credit card is nearly maxed, his car makes a noise he has consciously decided not to investigate, and the delivery app on his phone has just informed him that the last forty minutes of his life were worth $6.82.

Then, while scrolling through his phone at 1:53 in the afternoon, Ren sees a stupid hypothetical:

«Gain $1 every time you breathe in longer than you breathe out. Lose $1 if you breathe out longer than you breathe in.»

He taps the red pill.

The screen goes black.

A message appears.

[THE EXCHANGE HAS ACCEPTED YOUR SELECTION.]

Ren laughs.

Then he inhales.

Four seconds.

Exhales.

Three.

His phone vibrates.

BANK DEPOSIT: +$1.00

Ren stops breathing.

Not metaphorically.

For about eight seconds he genuinely forgets how.

## The Money-Breathing Art

The ability does exactly what it promised.

For every completed respiratory cycle:

- Inhale longer than exhale: +$1
- Exhale longer than inhale: -$1
- Equal durations produce nothing.

The money appears legally inside Ren's financial system.

Taxes exist.

Banks recognize it.

Government databases recognize it.

Every dollar has documentation.

There is no fraud to uncover because reality itself insists that Ren has legitimately earned the money.

At first, Ren thinks he has found an infinite-money glitch.

He hasn't.

He has awakened the most primitive form of a cultivation technique.
